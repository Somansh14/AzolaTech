Essential Travel Info

Plan your trip with confidence.

  • Best Time to Visit: September to November (Spring) or March to May (Autumn) for comfortable weather.
  • Currency: Australian Dollar (AUD). Card payments are widely accepted everywhere.
  • Time Zones: Australia spans 3 main time zones; plan your domestic flights accordingly.

Sample Itinerary

A glimpse into a 10-day classic Australian experience

Days 1-3: Sydney Highlights

Arrive in Sydney. Enjoy a private tour of the Sydney Opera House, climb the Harbour Bridge, and relax at Bondi Beach.

Days 4-6: The Great Barrier Reef

Fly to Cairns. Spend your days snorkeling or scuba diving in the Great Barrier Reef, and exploring the Daintree Rainforest.

Days 7-8: The Outback

Travel to Uluru (Ayers Rock). Experience a magical sunset dinner in the desert and learn about Aboriginal culture.

Days 9-10: Melbourne & Departure

Conclude your trip in cultural Melbourne. Explore the laneways, enjoy world-class coffee, and take a day trip to the Great Ocean Road before departing.
