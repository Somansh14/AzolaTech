Everything you need to know before you go.

  • Currency: New Zealand Dollar (NZD).
  • Getting Around: Self-driving is highly recommended to enjoy the scenic routes at your own pace.

Sample Itinerary

An unforgettable 12-day journey across both islands

Days 1-3: Auckland & Hobbiton

Arrive in Auckland. Pick up your premium rental and drive to Matamata for a magical tour of the Hobbiton Movie Set.

Days 4-5: Rotorua Geothermal Wonders

Experience Maori culture and witness bubbling mud pools and erupting geysers. Enjoy a relaxing evening at a luxury thermal spa.

Days 6-8: Queenstown Adrenaline

Fly to the South Island. Dive into adventure with bungee jumping, jet boating, and a scenic helicopter tour over the Remarkables.

Days 9-12: Milford Sound & Glaciers

Take a day trip to the breathtaking Milford Sound for a nature cruise. Continue up the west coast to explore the Franz Josef Glacier before departing from Christchurch.
