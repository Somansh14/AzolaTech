Essential Travel Info

Navigate the continent like a seasoned traveler.

  • Best Time to Visit: May-June or September-October for pleasant weather and fewer crowds.
  • Currency: Euro (€) in most countries (Swiss Francs in Switzerland).
  • Border Control: Traveling between Schengen countries is seamless with no internal border checks.

Sample Itinerary

A classic 14-day romance and culture tour

Days 1-4: Paris, France

Arrive in the City of Light. Enjoy VIP access to the Louvre, a private Seine river cruise at night, and a day trip to the Palace of Versailles.

Days 5-7: Swiss Alps, Switzerland

Take the high-speed train to Interlaken. Board the Glacier Express for panoramic alpine views, and visit the Top of Europe at Jungfraujoch.

Days 8-10: Venice & Florence, Italy

Travel to Venice for a romantic sunset gondola ride. Continue to Florence to marvel at Renaissance art and indulge in authentic Tuscan cuisine.

Days 11-14: Rome, Italy

Conclude your journey in Rome. Step back in time with private tours of the Colosseum and the Vatican, before catching your premium flight home.
